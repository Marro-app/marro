1. Who we are

Marro ([MARRO, INC.], “we,” “us”) is a web application that helps medical students plan and track their budgets. You can reach us any time at hello@joinmarro.com.

2. What we collect

In short: Your Google basics, your school, and the budget data you type in. We don’t connect to your bank or take payment details, and we don’t track you across other sites.

3. How we use it

In short: To run the app, keep it secure, improve it, and communicate with you.

We use your information to: log you in and operate the app; save your budget and sync it across your devices; show you your own numbers; keep the Service secure and prevent fraud and abuse; improve the Service and build new features; create anonymous group-level insights (Section 4); communicate with you about the Service; and comply with law and enforce our Terms.

4. Learning from students as a group

In short: Some of what makes Marro useful is the bigger picture across many students. That’s anonymous, group-level data that can’t identify you — and we may use or share it, including with partners. Your own records stay private.

Some of what makes Marro useful comes from the bigger picture — things like what a typical month costs for students at a school, or how aid packages tend to shape budgets. That bigger picture is aggregated, de-identified data: group patterns across many students, never any one person’s numbers, from which you cannot reasonably be identified. We use it to build better features, to show helpful comparisons (like how your budget compares to students in your class year), for research, and to work with partner organizations. Because this data does not identify anyone, we may use, share, license, or sell it for these purposes, and we own it. When we maintain de-identified data we take reasonable measures to keep it from being linked to any individual, commit to keep it in de-identified form and not to re-identify it, and require partners to do the same. Your own records always stay private to your account, and we never sell your personal information.

5. Do we sell or share your information?

In short: We never sell your personal information, and we don’t “share” it for cross-site advertising. We may sell or share anonymous group-level data, which isn’t personal information.

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not “share” your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined by California law. We may sell or license Aggregated Data (Section 4), which is not personal information and cannot identify you. We honor opt-out preference signals such as the Global Privacy Control (see Section 11). We do not disclose your individual financial or educational records in identifiable form to advertisers or data brokers.

6. Who we share it with

In short: A small number of service providers that help run Marro, plus legal or business-transfer situations.

We use a small number of service providers that process data only to provide their service to us under contract:

  • Google — sign-in (authentication).
  • Supabase — database and authentication backend (hosted on Amazon Web Services).
  • Vercel — website hosting.

If and when we add artificial-intelligence features, an AI model provider (for example, Anthropic) would process the information you submit on your behalf to generate the output you request, and would be contractually restricted from using it to train its own models except as disclosed. We may also disclose information to comply with law or lawful requests, to enforce our Terms, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets. Otherwise, we don’t share your personal information with third parties.

7. Cookies & local storage

In short: Only what’s needed to keep you signed in and let the app work offline. No advertising trackers.

Marro stores a login token and small preferences (like your theme and a cached copy of your budget) in your browser’s local storage. These are necessary for the app to function and to work offline. We don’t use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

8. Your Google data

Marro’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We only request basic profile and email scopes, and use that data solely to provide the sign-in and account features described above.

9. How long we keep it & deletion

In short: As long as your account exists. You can delete it any time; anonymous group data already created stays.

We keep your personal information for as long as your account exists and as needed to provide the Service, then for the period needed to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time by emailing hello@joinmarro.com, and we’ll remove it promptly, except information we’re permitted or required to keep and Aggregated Data already created (which no longer identifies you).

10. How we keep it safe

Your data is stored in a Supabase database (hosted on Amazon Web Services), protected by row-level security so it’s only readable by your own signed-in account; a copy is also cached locally in your browser so the app works offline. We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we can’t guarantee absolute security. Keep your credentials confidential and your devices secure.

11. Your privacy rights

In short: Depending on where you live (including California), you can access, correct, delete, or get a copy of your data, and opt out of any sale/share. We won’t discriminate against you for using these rights.

Depending on your state, you may have the right to: know and access the personal information we hold about you; delete it; correct it; obtain a portable copy; opt out of any sale or sharing; limit the use of sensitive personal information; and not be discriminated against for exercising your rights. Some rights are subject to legal exceptions.

To exercise these rights, email hello@joinmarro.com or use the controls in the app. We honor opt-out preference signals such as the Global Privacy Control (GPC) for the browser or device that sends them. We verify requests by matching the information you provide against our records and may ask for more information to confirm your identity; you may use an authorized agent, subject to verification. We respond within the timeframes required by law. Because we do not track you across other websites, Marro does not respond differently to browser “Do Not Track” signals beyond honoring recognized opt-out preference signals as described above.

12. Sensitive information

Some information you enter may be “sensitive personal information” under California law, and log-in credentials are treated as sensitive. We use it only to provide and secure the Service and for other purposes the law permits, and we do not use or disclose it to infer characteristics about you, and we do not sell it.

13. Children

Marro is intended for medical students and is not directed to anyone under 18. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if we learn we have, we’ll delete it. Contact us at hello@joinmarro.com if you believe a minor has provided us information.

14. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we’ll update this page and the “Last updated” date above and notify you as required by law. Continued use of Marro after a change means you accept the updated policy to the extent permitted by law.

15. Contact
